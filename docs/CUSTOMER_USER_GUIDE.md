---

### 2.2. Raising a Technical Complaint
If you experience an operational issue, bug, or service outage:
1. Click **Raise Complaint** from the sidebar or header.
2. Select an appropriate category (e.g. `Software Issue`, `Server Issue`, `Bug Report`, `Billing Issue`).
3. Select an incident priority:
   - **Critical (P1):** Complete system outage halting hospital or facility operations (4-hour SLA).
   - **High (P2):** Core module failure with significant operational impact (8-hour SLA).
   - **Medium (P3):** Intermittent issue or minor workflow impediment (24-hour SLA).
   - **Low (P4):** General inquiry or cosmetic adjustment (48-hour SLA).
4. Provide a descriptive subject and detailed summary.
5. Click **Submit Ticket**. A unique ticket ID (`DKI-2026-00001`) will be generated immediately.

---

### 2.3. Tracking Tickets & Confirming Resolution
1. Navigate to **Support Tickets** to view the full discussion history for your ticket.
2. Review replies from assigned Digikhojindia engineers.
3. Send replies and updates directly inside the ticket discussion thread.
4. Once the issue is resolved to your satisfaction, click **Confirm Resolution**.
5. Submit a **1 to 5 Star Rating** and operational feedback to help us maintain high service quality.

### 2.4. AMC Details & Lifetime Free Maintenance
Digikhojindia provides:
> **"Lifetime Free AMC – Subject to agreed contract terms."**

In the **AMC Details** view, you can:
- Review your active contract identifier (`AMC-2026-00001`).
- Verify contract validity dates.
- Inspect covered preventive maintenance tasks (weekly database integrity checks, OS updates, security patches).
- Review excluded services requiring separate change orders (new custom features, third-party SMS/cloud infrastructure fees).
- Access your dedicated 24x7 priority telephone helpline (`+91 99999 99999`).

### 2.5. Invoices & Billing Statements
In the **Invoices & Billing** tab:
- Review lifetime invoiced totals, payments reconciled, and current balance due.
- Inspect individual tax invoices formatted as `INV-2026-00001`.
- View detailed line item breakdowns and standard 18% GST (CGST 9% + SGST 9%).
- Click **View & Print** to print a hard copy or export a formatted PDF statement for your accounting department.
- Follow provided NEFT / RTGS / IMPS bank transfer and UPI details.
